Years shown

Reading across years. The City relabels departments and service areas from time to time — “Police” became “Police Department” in FY2027, and “Community Maintenance and Development” became “Community Maintenance.” Those are folded together here so each line stays continuous; the full list is in content/aliases.json. Two real accounting changes are not folded, because they moved money rather than renaming it: employee benefits were centralised into General Government in FY2026 (which is why Public Safety appears to fall that year), and Public Works was reorganised in FY2027. Compare the citywide total across years with confidence; compare an individual department across FY2025–FY2027 with care.

About this data

Source

All figures come directly from the City’s open data portal, data.cambridgema.gov — the same datasets the Budget Office already publishes:

  • Budget – Operating Expenditures
  • Budget – Operating Revenues
  • Budget – Capital

What these numbers are

These are budgeted amounts — the spending and revenue adopted in the City’s annual budget. They are not year-end actuals, and they are not a checkbook register of individual payments.

Cambridge’s fiscal year runs from July 1 to June 30. FY2027 means July 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027.

A note on precision

Some departments list multiple line items with identical descriptions in the same year. That is deliberate: the City tracks charges separately across departmental cost centers, and this view preserves the original structure rather than collapsing it.

Every yearly total is reconciled against the portal’s own figures when this page is built. If they disagree, the build fails rather than publishing numbers that don’t tie out.
