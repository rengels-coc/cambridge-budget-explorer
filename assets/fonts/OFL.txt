Fonts bundled with the Cambridge Budget Explorer
================================================

This page is set entirely in Noto Sans, one of the City of Cambridge brand
typefaces (Brand Guidelines, "Cambridge Reimagined", August 2024). A single
variable family covers all three brand roles by moving along its width axis:

    Headlines, big figures            Noto Sans, width axis at 80%
                                      (stands in for Cantabrigia)
    Body copy                         Noto Sans, width axis at 100%
    Tabs, buttons, links, captions    Noto Sans, width axis at 87.5%
                                      (this is Noto Sans Condensed)

Everything bundled here is under the SIL Open Font License, so this repository
carries no font-licensing restrictions and can be published freely.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
A note on Cantabrigia
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Cantabrigia BT, the City's own display face, is deliberately NOT bundled here.
It is licensed to the City by Bastarda Type Foundry: the City may embed it in
the City's own sites, applications and documents, but sublicensing or
redistribution to third parties is not permitted, and committing the files to a
public repository would be redistribution.

Condensed Noto Sans is the brand guidelines' own named substitute for it. If
this explorer ever moves onto City-controlled hosting and the Communications
Office would rather use the real face, add an @font-face block for it and put
"Cantabrigia" at the front of the --display stack in assets/css/style.css.
Nothing else has to change.
